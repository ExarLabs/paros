# Nutrition basics

Draft rules, not medical advice.

- **Before a long run:** something small and familiar 60 to 90 minutes before (toast with honey, a banana).
- **Runs over 90 minutes:** a gel or a few dates every 40 minutes, with water.
- **After:** a real meal within two hours, with protein.
- **Race week:** nothing new. The usual breakfast on race morning, tested on two long runs before.
