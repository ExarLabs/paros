# Second Tuesday Book Club

A small fictional reading group of six, meeting on the second Tuesday of each month at 19:00, usually in the library's community room.

## How the next book is chosen

1. Anyone can add a title to the shortlist in [[Reading list 2026]].
2. At each meeting the host proposes three from the shortlist.
3. A show of hands decides. The host breaks a tie.

## Hosting

Hosting rotates in alphabetical order of first names. The host books the room, brings the questions, and writes the meeting note.

## House rules

- Spoilers are fine: you are expected to have read it.
- Not finishing is fine too; say where you stopped.
- Bread is provided by the bakery (an unofficial perk).
