# Larkspur Bakery

A fictional neighbourhood bakery with two people (the owner and one baker) and a shop counter.

## What we sell

- Sourdough loaves (white, rye blend, seeded), baked daily
- Morning pastries: croissants, cardamom buns, one rotating special
- Wholesale bread for three local cafes (see [[Wholesale orders]])

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| Monday | Closed. Ordering, accounts, planning |
| Tuesday to Saturday | Bake from 04:30, shop open 07:00 to 14:00 |
| Thursday | Wholesale orders for the weekend are fixed at noon |
| Sunday | Shop open 08:00 to 12:00, pastries only |
