# Wholesale orders

All three customers are fictional.

## Terms

- **Cutoff:** orders for the next day by 12:00; weekend orders by Thursday 12:00.
- **Delivery:** Tuesday to Saturday, before 07:30, by bike trailer within the neighbourhood.
- **Minimum:** 6 loaves per delivery.
- **Payment:** invoiced monthly, 14 days.
