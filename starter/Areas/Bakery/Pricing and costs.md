# Pricing and costs

All figures are fictional and in an unnamed currency.

## Cost per white sourdough loaf (900 g)

| Part | Before | After the September flour change |
|---|---|---|
| Flour (550 g) | 0.52 | 0.63 |
| Salt, water, starter | 0.06 | 0.06 |
| Energy (oven share) | 0.38 | 0.38 |
| Packaging | 0.09 | 0.09 |
| **Total ingredients and energy** | **1.05** | **1.16** |

Labour is not in the table; it is covered by the margin.

## Margin target

- Shop price: at least 3 times ingredients and energy.
- Wholesale price: at least 2.8 times.

At 1.16 the wholesale white loaf needs 3.25 or more, so the October price of 3.30 holds the target. The rye blend and seeded loaves still need a recount (open task in `TODO.md`).
