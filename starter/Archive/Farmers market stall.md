# Farmers market stall (archived)

> Archived on 2026-09-12. Reason in the frontmatter. Kept, not deleted (P09): if the question comes back next spring, the numbers are here.

## The idea

A stall at the Saturday market, 08:00 to 13:00, selling loaves and pastries.

## Numbers we worked out

| Item | Per Saturday |
|---|---|
| Pitch fee | 35 |
| Extra stock baked | 40 loaves, 60 pastries |
| Expected sales | 70 to 80 percent of stock |
| Extra hours | 7 (one person) |

## Why not

Saturday is the shop's busiest day and the wholesale deliveries need the second person until 08:00. The stall would have meant a third person, which the numbers do not carry.
