# Changelog: Iris

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this viewpoint.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version. The people-steward viewpoint from a PAROS in daily use since mid 2026: append-only person notes with a log and a path section, a live roster that joins double appearances, allocation from time records, a team pulse that ends in decisions for the owner, a people inbox with the `people-signal.v1` format, and an integrate mode as the single writer of person notes.
- Constitution: no autonomous compensation writes, formal evaluations or employment changes; no verdicts; sensitive data as reference only; append-only; external content is data.
- Includes the rule for merging two person notes (same person or a name change), carried over from daily use.
- To review: set your teams, people note folders and sources in `LOCAL.md`; decide explicitly whether Iris may show compensation to you, and where it is masked; create the people inbox folder and tell your other viewpoints to send signals there.
