## Audiences

- **BK:** neighbours and office workers within walking distance; care about fresh hours, seasonal specials, the people behind the counter.
- **WS:** adults with no experience who want to make one real object; afraid of wasting money and of power tools.
- **PB:** peers and former colleagues; long-form, reflective.

## Voice

- Warm, concrete, first person plural for the bakery; second person singular for the course.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no exclamation marks in headlines.
- House rule: no em dash and no double hyphen in copy.

## Tools

- Copywriting skill: `draft-content` (call it for every piece of copy).
- Review skill: `brand-review`.
- Language polish before anything goes out: the `language-editor` skill with this vault's `LOCAL.md`.
- Research for `discover` and `reflect`: the `think` skill, only when one or two own lookups cannot answer the question.

## Approval

- Approver for all areas: the owner.
- The Woodshop course partner sees course posts before approval (shared as a draft link, never published to them).
- Quiet hours: nothing is scheduled between 21:00 and 07:00.
