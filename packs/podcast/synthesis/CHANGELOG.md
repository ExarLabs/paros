# Changelog: podcast-synthesis

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Single-episode synthesis from the full transcript and real analytics, batch mode with an approved order, tracking matrix and patterns file.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-008.
- To review: set your folders, template and analytics access in `LOCAL.md`; start with your three most viewed episodes.
