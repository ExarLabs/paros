# Changelog: podcast-cold-open

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Quick mode for hook ideas; full mode with blocklist first, a ten-candidate hook pool, at least five stacks with a thesis variant and a fallback, waveform-verified cut points, re-transcribed clips, audio previews, editor sheet, transition section and a paste-ready block.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-014. R-012 and R-013 are marked low evidence (R-013: the 30 to 40 second limit on the host introduction comes from one retention curve).
- To review: set your cold-open length, working folder and hand-off channel in `LOCAL.md`; check the host-introduction limit against your own retention curves and record what you find.
