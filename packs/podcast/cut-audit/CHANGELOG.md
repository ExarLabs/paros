# Changelog: podcast-cut-audit

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Saved transcript as evidence, an "is it already cut" test, promise consistency, callbacks versus duplicates, measured silences and cut points, a prioritized editor brief plus a paste-ready block.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-008, learned from an audit whose claims failed a later re-check (2026-09-07).
- To review: fill in your editor, hand-off channel and priority labels in `LOCAL.md`; if you have an older audit, re-check one of its claims against a saved transcript.
