# Changelog: podcast-title

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Five titles in the show format, paired with the chosen thumbnail, under 75 characters with the essence in the first 60, a universalization check for niche topics, ranking against measured patterns.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-007. The length rule was corrected from a rigid 60 after two independent accepted titles exceeded it.
- To review: write your title format and its mandatory parts into `LOCAL.md`; measure the length of your last accepted titles before keeping the default rule.
