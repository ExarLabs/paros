# Changelog: podcast-prep

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Two documents per episode (invitation and preparation questions), fixed show data from `LOCAL.md`, cross-reference to earlier episodes with the same guest or topic, a draft cover message that is never sent automatically.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-006, carried over from a capability in use since mid 2026.
- To review: copy `LOCAL.example.md` to `LOCAL.md`, fill in your location, intake form, folders and output formats, and run it once on your next guest.
