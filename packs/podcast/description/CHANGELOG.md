# Changelog: podcast-description

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Head and verbatim footer, a template derived from published descriptions, quoted hook with a keyword, related episodes most viewed first, optional guest contact block, checklist.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-009. R-007 is low evidence.
- To review: paste your footer into `LOCAL.md` word for word, derived from your best published description; check your last description against the checklist.
