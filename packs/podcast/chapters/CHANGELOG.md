# Changelog: podcast-chapters

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the podcast pack. Chapter timecodes from the edited cut, platform format, ten to twelve chapters or full coverage for list episodes, internal hook marks.
- Recipe and spice: everything that belongs to one show lives in `LOCAL.md`, copied from `LOCAL.example.md` (a made-up show).
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-006. R-005 (full coverage for list episodes) is low evidence, from one episode.
- To review: make sure your chapters come from the cut, not the raw recording; set separator and count in `LOCAL.md`.
