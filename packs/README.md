# Packs: skills for a whole workflow

A pack is a group of skills that together cover one recurring workflow, in order. Adopt a whole pack or only the skills you need; each skill follows the same rules as in [`skills/`](../skills/README.md) (recipe in `CURRENT.md`, your settings in `LOCAL.md`, updates as advice).

| Pack | Covers | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| [`podcast`](podcast/README.md) | Producing an episode: prep and guest invitation, cut audit, cold open, thumbnail before title, chapters, description, publishing; optional synthesis, channel intelligence, clip lab, listener inbox | 12 |
| [`meetings`](meetings/README.md) | Before, during and after a meeting: prep from the vault, transcription, intake (decisions, commitments, open questions), an adversarial second pass, archiving rules, follow-ups into your task list | 3 (plus `transcribe` and `adversarial-second-pass` from `skills/`) |

> Which PAROS packs fit my work, and what would adopting the meetings pack look like in my vault?
