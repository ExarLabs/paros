# Changelog: meeting-prep

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the meetings pack. A one-page prep note written into the meeting's future home under its stem: purpose, what changed since last time, commitments split into ours and theirs, open and done, questions to ask, sensitivities, a proposed agenda and a recording reminder; every fact linked to its source, gaps marked.
- New skill, built from the morning briefing and capture practices of a PAROS in daily use, and shaped to feed `meeting-intake` (which compares what came out with what went in).
- Constitution: read-only except the prep note, never contacts participants, facts only with sources, consent reminder for recording, private notes about people referenced by link only.
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-008.
- To review: copy `LOCAL.example.md` to `LOCAL.md`; name your areas, where state files and task lists live, and the recording consent rule that applies to you; decide whether the agent may read your calendar.
