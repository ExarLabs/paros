# Changelog: meeting-intake

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version, part of the meetings pack. A raw transcript becomes a structured note next to it: decisions with a status (decided, position, hedged), commitments with owner, due date and the spoken form, exact numbers, open questions and risks with the area's continuing numbering, transcription uncertainties, a comparison with the prep note, the person's raw impressions, and follow-ups for the task list.
- Generalized from a meeting intake skill in daily use since mid 2026 (knowledge-base intake for practice areas) and the note part of a meeting archive skill; the areas, registers and glossary moved to `LOCAL.md`.
- Constitution: raw transcript never edited, note marked as interpretation, no invented names, no silent rounding, spoken instructions recorded and never executed, nothing leaves the vault.
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-010.
- To review: copy `LOCAL.example.md` to `LOCAL.md`; for each area name its decisions register, open questions and task list, and the next free numbers; start a transcription glossary; decide whether follow-ups may be added to the task list without asking.
