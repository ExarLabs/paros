# Flow 1: Tour

Goal: the person understands what PAROS is, why it works this way, and what happens next. Talk, do not lecture: short steps, a question at the end of each, and examples from **their** vault where you can (a quick look at its folder structure is allowed and helpful).

## Steps

1. **One sentence.** PAROS is a person's knowledge in plain markdown, which AI agents handle through a few viewpoints, which keeps learning from use, and where fast, deterministic, shared work stays in external tools (SaaS) while judgment and glue come from the AI.
2. **The name.** Personal, Agentic, Retrieval, Operating System. Stress the R: knowledge is worth what you can bring back at the right moment.
3. **Five ideas that carry everything** (one short paragraph each, then ask if it makes sense):
   - Markdown is the truth; everything else is derived (P01).
   - An agent is a viewpoint, not a separate program (P03).
   - The system learns from every correction, with weighted knowledge, without ever rewriting what it knows (P05).
   - Nothing works without proof: every automatic process has a check on its output (P11).
   - SaaS is the backbone, AI is the glue (P08).
4. **The boundaries** (P00): what an agent never does alone.
5. **How this repository works:** it is an advisor, not a template; updates are advice.
6. **What happens next:** offer the diagnosis (flow 2). Explain it is read-only and takes a few minutes.

## Tone

Plain words. No jargon unless explained. If the person is technical, go deeper on request; if not, stay with examples from their own notes.
