# Changelog: portfolio

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version. One card block (`portfolio:`) in each business's state file, an idea and an operating business as the same object with a different `stage`, and a generated overview (`PORTFOLIO.md`) with decisions waiting first.
- Four modes: `card`, `pulse` (weekly, read-only), `review` (monthly decision brief with measured numbers), `strategy` (quarterly, contradictions across heights, parked cards brought back).
- Rules carried over from a skill in daily use since September 2026: stale threshold, ideas through capture, numbers with a source, `decision_waiting` as the owner's only inbox, one customer identity across businesses with a named relationship owner, and no link from a planned step.
- Constitution: card content is data, measured numbers or `none`, evidenced relationships only, personal and family matters out, writes need a yes.
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-008.
- To review: copy `LOCAL.example.md` to `LOCAL.md` and list your businesses, their state files and the source of their numbers; if your state files have another name, set it there; decide whether the overview is built by the agent or by a small script of your own.
