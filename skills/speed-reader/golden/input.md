**The Case for Boring Meetings** by R. Example (2025), an 800 word essay in three sections.

*1. The problem.* Teams that try to make every meeting engaging spend their energy on format instead of decisions. In a survey of 40 teams the author ran, the teams with the most varied meeting formats also reported the most "decisions revisited later".

*2. The proposal.* Use the same agenda every time: what changed, what is blocked, what we decide, who does what by when. "A meeting should be as predictable as a train timetable." Novelty belongs in workshops, not in the weekly meeting.

*3. Objections.* People will get bored. The author's answer: boredom is the price of focus, and a 25 minute meeting is short enough that boredom does not set in. "If people are bored, the meeting is too long, not too plain."
