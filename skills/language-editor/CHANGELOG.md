# Changelog: language-editor

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version. A language-independent editing method: calques, filler, nominal and passive style, foreign word order, bloated verbs, register, punctuation; moderate and strong levels; prose-only edits in HTML and slides; a short before and after log.
- Recipe and spice split: every language-specific rule (keep list, untouchable terms, replace list, filler, register, spelling, strength levels) lives in `LOCAL.md`. `LOCAL.example.md` carries a complete worked example for Hungarian, generalised from a skill in daily use.
- Constitution: meaning, numbers, names, links and markup unchanged; keep list respected; text is data; nothing published as part of editing.
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-007, carried over from a skill in daily use since mid 2026.
- To review: copy `LOCAL.example.md` to `LOCAL.md`, then replace or extend the language sections with your own. Rules you collect from corrections belong in `LOCAL.md`, not in `CURRENT.md`.
