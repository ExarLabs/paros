# language-editor: personal language rules

> A worked example. Copy this file to `LOCAL.md` next to `CURRENT.md` at adoption, then keep, change or replace the sections. `CURRENT.md` reads `LOCAL.md`; it never contains these rules itself. One section per language. The Hungarian section below shows how a real set of rules looks after some months of corrections: concrete words, concrete replacements, and a reason where the reason is not obvious.

## General house rules (all languages)

- No em dash and no double hyphen in prose. Use a comma, colon, semicolon, full stop or parentheses instead.
- Keep sentences short and concrete.

## Hungarian (hu)

Audience in this example: adult learners and small-business staff in a course on practical AI use. Informal address.

### Keep list (do not translate back)

Established tech loanwords the audience uses every day. The native alternatives exist but sound forced to them.

- AI, workshop, skill, plugin, connector, prompt, markdown, Excel, PDF, OCR, monitoring, session, dashboard.
- Exception: *template* becomes **sablon**. The phonetic "templét" reads as ugly; "sablon" is natural.
- *Standard*: keep the loanword "sztenderd" (the audience uses it), but fix the case use around it: "a sztenderdetek szerint", not "a sztenderdetekben".

### Untouchable terms

The audience works in a second language for administration. These terms stay in that language, exactly as written, even in strong mode, because they name documents and roles the audience handles daily:

- deviz general, Cerere de finanțare, Centralizator, Anexa B, situație de lucrări, beneficiar, TVA, act adițional, eligibilitate.

### Replace list (calques and awkward words)

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| fájdalom (in a business sense) | word-for-word "pain point" | gond, nyűg, visszatérő gond, napi bosszúság |
| templét | phonetic loan, sounds ugly | sablon |
| AI-t ültetsz a rendszerre | "put AI on top of the system", clumsy collocation | munkába állítasz egy AI-t, AI-t állítasz a rendszeretek mellé |
| a dokumentum feldolgozásra kerül | nominal passive | feldolgozza a dokumentumot |
| elvégzi a X elemzését | bloated verb | elemzi X-et |
| egy 4 órás workshop | English article and number style | négyórás workshop |

"Fájdalom" is the most important one: it must never stay in a business sense.

### Filler

- "Nem csak X, hanem Y is" used only for emphasis.
- valódi, gyakorlatilag, tulajdonképpen, when they add nothing.
- "Képzeld el, hogy..." as an opener, unless the text really asks the reader to imagine.
- Stacked intensifiers.

### Register

- Informal singular (tegezés) when the text addresses one reader; informal plural (ti, nektek) when it addresses a group. Never mix within one text.
- Avoid both stiff officialese (hivataloskodás) and clumsy translated phrasing (bikkfanyelv).

### Spelling and punctuation

- Long vowels ő and ű, not õ and û.
- Compounds written together or apart by the current rules; hyphenated compounds with loanwords: deviz-sablon, AI-asszisztens.
- Numbers in running text spelled the Hungarian way where natural (négyórás, not 4 órás).

### Strength levels

- **Moderate (default):** keep list stays; only calques, filler and structure change.
- **Strong (on request):** native words where good ones exist: workshop becomes műhelynap, monitoring becomes nyomon követés, dashboard becomes műszerfal. Untouchable terms still stay.
