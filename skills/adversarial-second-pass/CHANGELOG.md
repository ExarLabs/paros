# Changelog: adversarial-second-pass

Every entry: what changed, and **what to review in a vault that has already adopted this skill.**

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

- First public version. A single fresh-context reviewer rereads the full source before the result, looks only for omissions and errors (commitments, unnamed risks, guesses recorded as decisions, attribution, numbers, extraction errors, implicit signals), and returns evidence-backed JSON; high and medium findings are integrated, contractual or committed changes wait for the person's yes.
- Generalized from a meeting-notes review to any important result built from a source.
- Constitution: reviewer is not the author, refutes rather than summarises, evidence for every finding, no canonical change without a yes, no invented names.
- Pitfalls R-001 to R-008, carried over from a skill in daily use since mid 2026.
- To review: decide which of your outputs count as high stakes and name them in your vault entry file or in this skill's "When to use"; check that your platform can start a fresh-context reviewer (subagent or new session).
